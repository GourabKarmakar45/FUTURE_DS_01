# 📊 Business Sales Performance Analytics

## 🔍 Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end analysis of business sales data to uncover key insights and support data-driven decision-making. It combines Python-based data analysis with an interactive Power BI dashboard, simulating a real-world business analytics workflow.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze revenue trends over time
* Identify top-performing products
* Understand customer purchasing behavior
* Evaluate operational efficiency (delivery & payments)
* Build a professional, client-ready dashboard

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Python**: Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn
* **Power BI**: Data visualization and dashboard creation
* **Dataset**: CSV (Sales Data)

---

## 📊 Dashboard Highlights

* 📌 KPI Cards: Total Revenue, Total Orders, Quantity Sold, Average Order Value
* 📈 Revenue Trend Analysis (Time-based insights)
* 🏆 Top 5 Products by Revenue
* 💳 Payment Method Distribution
* 🚚 Delivery Status Analysis
* 🎛️ Interactive Filters (Slicers for Date, Product, Payment Method)

---

## 📈 Key Insights

* A small group of products contributes to the majority of total revenue
* Sales show noticeable fluctuations over time, indicating trend patterns
* Certain payment methods are preferred by customers
* Delivery status significantly impacts customer satisfaction and business efficiency

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Focus marketing strategies on high-performing products
* Optimize inventory management based on demand trends
* Improve delivery processes to reduce delays and cancellations
* Encourage use of preferred payment methods
* Use data insights for strategic decision-making
